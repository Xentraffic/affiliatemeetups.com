New York City

United States · 1 event tracked

New York in July for Affiliate Summit East is a rite of passage for North American performance marketers. The New York Marriott Marquis in Times Square is an iconic venue, and the city's energy amplifies every conversation, dinner, and after-party into something memorable.

Affiliate Community Context

ASE is the flagship US-market affiliate conference, drawing 4,500+ from insurance, lead gen, ecommerce, nutra, and email marketing. NYC's density of media buyers, agency operators, and brand-side marketers makes it a unique conversation type compared to Western events. The city itself functions as a giant side event — rooftop bars, private dinners, and penthouse parties run around the clock.

Practical Info for Affiliates

Times Square is the center of the action — the Marriott Marquis is walking distance from most midtown hotels. July in NYC is hot and busy; book accommodation months in advance. Uber, Lyft, and the subway handle transportation. Budget: NYC is expensive. Dinner and drinks for a group can run $500+ easily.
