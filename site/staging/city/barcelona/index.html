Barcelona

Spain · 1 event tracked

Barcelona is a natural affiliate conference city: sunny, beautiful, cheap to fly into from anywhere in Europe, and home to two major recurring affiliate events — Affiliate World Europe (when it was here pre-2024) and iGB Affiliate. The city's infrastructure for large events is excellent.

Affiliate Community Context

AMU has confirmed presence in Barcelona 2023 (photos verified). The city is particularly strong for iGaming affiliates — iGB Affiliate at Fira Gran Via draws the most focused iGaming affiliate audience in Europe. January timing makes it the opening event of the affiliate calendar year. Affiliate World ran here prior to moving to Budapest.

Practical Info for Affiliates

Fira Gran Via is in L'Hospitalet de Llobregat, a metro ride from central Barcelona. Most attendees stay in Eixample or along the Diagonal. Side events spread across Gothic Quarter, Barceloneta (beach bars), and high-end Eixample restaurants. Getting an Airbnb for event week is significantly cheaper than hotels.
