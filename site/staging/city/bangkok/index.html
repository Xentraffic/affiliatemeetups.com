Bangkok

Thailand · 1 event tracked

Bangkok is the global hub of affiliate marketing in Asia. If you run traffic, sell supplements, or operate affiliate programs in any major vertical, Bangkok is the city where you'll find the highest concentration of active operators per square kilometer.

Affiliate Community Context

Affiliate World Asia draws 7,000+ to the Centara Grand each December, but Bangkok's affiliate community extends year-round. Digital nomads, ecommerce operators, and media buyers have made Sukhumvit and Silom their long-term bases. AMU has confirmed presence at AW Asia Bangkok in 2023 and December 2024 at Hard Rock Cafe Bangkok — one of the community's most-attended events.

Practical Info for Affiliates

December in Bangkok is peak season — book accommodation early. The Centara Grand is in CentralWorld, easy access from BTS Skytrain. Side events spread across rooftop bars in Silom and Sukhumvit. Thai baht makes everything affordable for Western budgets. Grab is the go-to for rides.
