Lisbon

Portugal · 1 event tracked

Lisbon has emerged as Europe's hottest tech and gaming conference city, and SBC Summit's mega-event at FIL and MEO Arena confirms its status. Warm October weather, affordable costs (for Western Europe), and a laid-back culture make it a favorite for post-conference decompression.

Affiliate Community Context

SBC Summit draws 40,000+ to Lisbon for iGaming and sports betting. The affiliate track is a significant segment. Performance marketers with iGaming exposure will find it dense with operator buyers. The MEO Arena closing party is a highlight of the iGaming conference circuit. No confirmed AMU presence at SBC Lisbon.

Practical Info for Affiliates

FIL is in Parque das Nações, a 20-min metro from central Lisbon. The venue is modern and large. Side events spread across Parque das Nações and the historic Alfama and Bairro Alto neighborhoods. Portuguese cuisine and wine are excellent and affordable.
