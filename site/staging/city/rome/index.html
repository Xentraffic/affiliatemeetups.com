Rome

Italy · 1 event tracked

Rome hosts SiGMA World 2026 at Fiera Roma, marking the flagship iGaming conference's move from Malta to Italy's capital. A city of historic grandeur with a thriving modern startup and tech scene — an event backdrop unlike any other in the circuit.

Affiliate Community Context

SiGMA Rome 2026 (November 2–5) is co-hosted with the Affiliate Grand Slam (AGS), making it a two-in-one opportunity for iGaming and broader performance marketers. AGS specifically targets affiliate and performance marketing relationships in the iGaming space. SiGMA Rome is listed on the AMU homepage under upcoming events — attendance TBC.

Practical Info for Affiliates

Fiera Roma is southwest of the city center, accessible by car or bus. Most attendees will stay in EUR or Trastevere. Rome has a robust side-event culture, and conference organizers typically run evening networking events in the city's historic venues. November is off-peak for tourism — prices are lower, the city is less crowded.
