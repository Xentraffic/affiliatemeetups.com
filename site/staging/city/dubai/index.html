Dubai

United Arab Emirates · 1 event tracked

Dubai has positioned itself as the global super-affiliate hub, particularly for eight-figure operators and luxury-lifestyle affiliate brands. Tax-free income, world-class hotels, and a business-friendly environment have drawn a disproportionate concentration of top performers.

The affiliate community here skews higher-budget than most other cities.

Practical Info for Affiliates

Dubai venues are world-class. Events typically run at hotels on Sheikh Zayed Road or in DIFC. Everything is accessible by taxi or Careem. July–August is extremely hot — winter months (November–March) are ideal. The city runs on late schedules; dinner at 9pm, parties until 4am.
