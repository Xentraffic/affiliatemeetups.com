Affiliate World Europe 2026

📍 Budapest, Hungary

Affiliate World Europe is widely considered the premier gathering for performance marketers in Europe. Held annually in Budapest since at least 2024, it pulls together affiliate marketers, media buyers, ecommerce operators, ad networks, and traffic sources from over 110 countries. The conference runs two full days of speaker sessions across multiple stages, a large expo floor with 300+ exhibitors, and niche-focused networking mixers (Meta, Google, TikTok, AI). The real action often happens off-stage — rooftop parties, side events, and post-session hallway conversations are where partnerships actually form. For nutra, ecom, and lead-gen affiliates, Budapest in July is one of the highest-ROI trips of the year in terms of deal density per dollar spent.

Affiliate Summit East 2026

📍 New York City, United States

Affiliate Summit East is one of the longest-running and most-attended affiliate marketing conferences in North America. The event brings together performance marketers, affiliates, networks, advertisers, and technology suppliers at the New York Marriott Marquis in Times Square. ASE skews toward US-centric verticals — insurance, finance, health & wellness, lead-gen, and email marketing. The marketplace floor is dense with decision-makers, and the side-event scene around Manhattan is often as valuable as the conference itself. For anyone operating in lead generation, email, or US-market affiliate offers, ASE is a mandatory stop.

PI LIVE Europe 2026

📍 London, United Kingdom

PI LIVE Europe is the UK's leading performance marketing and partnerships conference, hosted at Old Billingsgate in London. The event brings together brands, publishers, agencies, affiliate networks, and technology platforms focused on the partnership economy. Affiliate World Asia 2026

📍 Bangkok, Thailand

Affiliate World Asia is the largest edition of the AW franchise, drawing 7,000+ attendees from over 110 countries to Bangkok in December. The Centara Grand venue is one of Bangkok's premier conference facilities, and the city itself has become the de facto capital of global affiliate marketing — cheap, accessible, and surrounded by a massive expat operator community. AWA covers the full stack: Meta and social media ads, search, ecommerce, AI tools, and the huge affiliate and CPA network ecosystem active across SEA. AMU has a proven track record in Bangkok with events at Hard Rock Cafe Bangkok. December is a natural gathering point before the holiday break.

📍 Rome, Italy
