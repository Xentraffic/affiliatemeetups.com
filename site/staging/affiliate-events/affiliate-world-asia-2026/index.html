Affiliate World Asia 2026

📅 December 9–10, 2026 📍 Bangkok, Thailand 🏛 Centara Grand & Bangkok Convention Centre

About This Event

Affiliate World Asia is the largest edition of the AW franchise, drawing 7,000+ attendees from over 110 countries to Bangkok in December. The Centara Grand venue is one of Bangkok's premier conference facilities, and the city itself has become the de facto capital of global affiliate marketing — cheap, accessible, and surrounded by a massive expat operator community. AWA covers the full stack: Meta and social media ads, search, ecommerce, AI tools, and the huge affiliate and CPA network ecosystem active across SEA. AMU has a proven track record in Bangkok with events at Hard Rock Cafe Bangkok. December is a natural gathering point before the holiday break.

Who Attends?

  • Affiliate marketers running paid traffic (Meta, Google, native, push)
  • Ecommerce operators and DTC brand founders
  • Affiliate networks and offer owners
  • Media buyers across nutra, finance, and lead gen
  • Traffic source representatives (ad networks, DSPs)
  • Software vendors: tracking, analytics, creative tools

Which Verticals Dominate?

  • Nutra and health supplements (huge buyer presence)
  • Ecommerce and DTC brands
  • Finance and credit card offers
  • Software and SaaS affiliate programs
  • Education and online courses

Worth Attending for Affiliates and Media Buyers?

With 7000+ attendees across Thailand and international markets, this is a high-density networking opportunity. The ROI on conference attendance is almost entirely driven by who's in the room — at this scale, you can expect a critical mass of active buyers, sellers, and decision-makers.

The real value is typically in the side events and hallway conversations that surround the main conference program. Come with a meeting list prepared in advance — don't rely on walking the floor and hoping.

Networking and Side Events

  • Pre-conference networking events typically start the day before the main show
  • After-parties, brand-sponsored dinners, and suite meetings run throughout
  • Private "invite only" gatherings are where the highest-value connections form — position yourself early
  • AW-specific niche Mixers (Meta, Google, TikTok, AI) are structured networking formats within the conference
  • Bangkok after-dark scene: rooftop bars, hotel terraces, and venue-hopping are deeply embedded in the AW Asia tradition
