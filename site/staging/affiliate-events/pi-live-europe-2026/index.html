PI LIVE Europe 2026

📅 October 19–21, 2026 📍 London, United Kingdom 🏛 Old Billingsgate, London

About This Event

PI LIVE Europe is the UK's leading performance marketing and partnerships conference, hosted at Old Billingsgate in London. The event brings together brands, publishers, agencies, affiliate networks, and technology platforms focused on the partnership economy. It's more brand-safe and agency-heavy than events like Affiliate World or ASE, making it particularly valuable for networks and advertisers seeking premium publisher and influencer relationships. The pre-event party on October 19 kicks things off, with the main conference days running through October 21. Strong for retail, travel, and financial services affiliate programs.

Who Attends?

  • Affiliate marketers running paid traffic (Meta, Google, native, push)
  • Ecommerce operators and DTC brand founders
  • Affiliate networks and offer owners
  • Media buyers across nutra, finance, and lead gen
  • Traffic source representatives (ad networks, DSPs)
  • Software vendors: tracking, analytics, creative tools

Which Verticals Dominate?

  • Nutra and health supplements (huge buyer presence)
  • Ecommerce and DTC brands
  • Finance and credit card offers
  • Software and SaaS affiliate programs
  • Education and online courses

Worth Attending for Affiliates and Media Buyers?

This event draws a curated, focused audience rather than the biggest crowd. That's often a feature, not a bug — smaller events tend to have higher deal-making density per conversation, less noise, and more genuine networking.

The real value is typically in the side events and hallway conversations that surround the main conference program. Come with a meeting list prepared in advance — don't rely on walking the floor and hoping.

Networking and Side Events

  • Pre-conference networking events typically start the day before the main show
  • After-parties, brand-sponsored dinners, and suite meetings run throughout
  • Private "invite only" gatherings are where the highest-value connections form — position yourself early
