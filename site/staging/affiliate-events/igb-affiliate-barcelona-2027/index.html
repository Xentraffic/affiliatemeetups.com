iGB Affiliate Barcelona 2027

📅 January 19–20, 2027 📍 Barcelona, Spain 🏛 Fira Gran Via, Barcelona

About This Event

iGB Affiliate Barcelona is the home conference of the iGaming affiliate community — focused squarely on connecting iGaming operators with the affiliates who drive their player acquisition. Fira Gran Via is a world-class exhibition space, and the January timing makes this the opening marquee event of the European affiliate calendar. The Barcelona affiliate scene has a long history with AMU — AMU has confirmed presence at previous Barcelona events. If your business includes iGaming traffic, casino, or sports betting affiliates, iGB Affiliate is the single most-targeted event of the year. The adjacent networking scene in Barcelona (parties, rooftop meetings, agency dinners) is second to none in Europe.

Who Attends?

  • iGaming affiliates and webmaster SEO operators
  • Casino and sportsbook operators
  • Payment processing and fraud prevention providers
  • Licensing and compliance consultants
  • Affiliate program managers and affiliate networks
  • Performance marketers with iGaming traffic exposure

Which Verticals Dominate?

  • Online casino and slots
  • Sports betting and esports wagering
  • Poker and skill-games platforms
  • Lottery and scratch card programs
  • Regulated and grey-market operations across key jurisdictions

Worth Attending for Affiliates and Media Buyers?

This event draws a curated, focused audience rather than the biggest crowd. That's often a feature, not a bug — smaller events tend to have higher deal-making density per conversation, less noise, and more genuine networking.

The real value is typically in the side events and hallway conversations that surround the main conference program. Come with a meeting list prepared in advance — don't rely on walking the floor and hoping.

Networking and Side Events

  • Pre-conference networking events typically start the day before the main show
  • After-parties, brand-sponsored dinners, and suite meetings run throughout
  • Private "invite only" gatherings are where the highest-value connections form — position yourself early
