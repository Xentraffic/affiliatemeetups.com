LeadsCon Las Vegas 2027

📅 April 5–7, 2027 📍 Las Vegas, United States 🏛 MGM Grand, Las Vegas

About This Event

LeadsCon is the world's most focused lead generation conference, pulling together lead buyers, sellers, technology platforms, and performance marketers for three days at the MGM Grand. Where ASW covers everything, LeadsCon goes deep: insurance lead gen, mortgage, solar, home services, finance, and the tech stack that drives ping-tree and call center ecosystems. If you're running lead gen campaigns in US verticals — insurance, home improvement, finance — LeadsCon is where the buyers you need are. Ringba, call center operators, and pay-per-call networks are heavily represented. The April timing gives you a first-half checkpoint after ASW.

Who Attends?

  • Lead buyers in insurance, finance, mortgage, home services
  • Lead sellers and publishers driving inbound calls
  • Call center operators and ping-tree technology companies
  • Pay-per-call networks and performance marketing agencies
  • CRM, compliance, and lead validation technology vendors

Which Verticals Dominate?

  • Auto and home insurance (primary vertical)
  • Mortgage and home equity leads
  • Solar and home improvement
  • Personal finance and credit
  • Health insurance and Medicare

Worth Attending for Affiliates and Media Buyers?

This event draws a curated, focused audience rather than the biggest crowd. That's often a feature, not a bug — smaller events tend to have higher deal-making density per conversation, less noise, and more genuine networking.

The real value is typically in the side events and hallway conversations that surround the main conference program. Come with a meeting list prepared in advance — don't rely on walking the floor and hoping.

Networking and Side Events

  • Pre-conference networking events typically start the day before the main show
  • After-parties, brand-sponsored dinners, and suite meetings run throughout
  • Private "invite only" gatherings are where the highest-value connections form — position yourself early
  • Las Vegas runs 24/7 — poolside meetings, casino-floor conversations, and late-night suite gatherings extend the networking window indefinitely
