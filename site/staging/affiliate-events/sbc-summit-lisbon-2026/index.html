SBC Summit Lisbon 2026

📅 September 29 – October 1, 2026 📍 Lisbon, Portugal 🏛 FIL — Feira Internacional de Lisboa & MEO Arena

About This Event

SBC Summit is the flagship event in the SBC calendar and one of the largest gaming and sports betting conferences in the world, reportedly drawing 40,000+ professionals. Held at the FIL and MEO Arena in Lisbon, it covers the full spectrum of gambling tech: sportsbook, casino, payments, compliance, and affiliate marketing. The affiliate track is significant — iGaming affiliate programs, operators, and networks use SBC to meet, renegotiate deals, and scout new traffic sources. Performance marketers with iGaming or sports betting exposure will find it dense with buyers. Closes October 1 with the INFINITY Closing Party.

Who Attends?

  • iGaming affiliates and webmaster SEO operators
  • Casino and sportsbook operators
  • Payment processing and fraud prevention providers
  • Licensing and compliance consultants
  • Affiliate program managers and affiliate networks
  • Performance marketers with iGaming traffic exposure

Which Verticals Dominate?

  • Online casino and slots
  • Sports betting and esports wagering
  • Poker and skill-games platforms
  • Lottery and scratch card programs
  • Regulated and grey-market operations across key jurisdictions

Worth Attending for Affiliates and Media Buyers?

With 40000+ attendees across Portugal and international markets, this is a high-density networking opportunity. The ROI on conference attendance is almost entirely driven by who's in the room — at this scale, you can expect a critical mass of active buyers, sellers, and decision-makers.

The real value is typically in the side events and hallway conversations that surround the main conference program. Come with a meeting list prepared in advance — don't rely on walking the floor and hoping.

Networking and Side Events

  • Pre-conference networking events typically start the day before the main show
  • After-parties, brand-sponsored dinners, and suite meetings run throughout
  • Private "invite only" gatherings are where the highest-value connections form — position yourself early
